## Proficiency Levels Explained

### Awareness (Orange Badge)
- **What it means**: You're beginning to recognize this competency
- **What's next**: Focus on noticing when this skill is needed
- **Growth focus**: Build awareness and basic understanding

### Developing (Amber Badge)
- **What it means**: You're building skill and consistency
- **What's next**: Practice applying the skill more regularly
- **Growth focus**: Strengthen technique and frequency

### Proficient (Blue Badge)
- **What it means**: You demonstrate solid mastery
- **What's next**: Sustain your practice and deepen impact
- **Growth focus**: Consistency and helping others learn

### Advanced (Green Badge)
- **What it means**: You show exemplary capability
- **What's next**: Model the skill and mentor others
- **Growth focus**: Leadership and systems-level impact

## BRAVIN Core Sequence

This is the foundational pattern for emotionally intelligent leadership:

1. **NOTICE** → Recognize emotional signals, tension, silence
2. **NAME** → Articulate what's happening ("I notice...")
3. **HOLD** → Stay with discomfort, don't rush to fix
4. **ALIGN** → Connect to shared values and purpose
5. **REPAIR** → Take visible action to rebuild trust

### Example in Practice

**Scenario**: Team member is quiet in a meeting

**NOTICE**: "Sam hasn't spoken and their body language seems closed off"

**NAME**: "Sam, I notice you've been quiet. I'm wondering if something doesn't feel right"

**HOLD**: *Wait in silence, don't fill it with more words*

**ALIGN**: "Our team value is candid feedback. What's on your mind?"

**REPAIR**: "Thank you for sharing that concern. Here's what I'll do about it..."

## Common Questions

**Q: Why didn't I score "Advanced" on everything?**
A: Advanced proficiency requires consistent, sophisticated application. Most learners develop gradually through the levels. Focus on growth, not perfection.

**Q: How do I improve my proficiency level?**
A: Follow the growth actions provided, practice the BRAVIN sequence, and complete more simulations. Development takes time and deliberate practice.

**Q: Are some competencies more important than others?**
A: All five competencies work together. Trust building and emotional intelligence are foundational; adaptive communication helps you deliver effectively; ethical leadership and values-based decisions guide your choices.

**Q: Can I share this feedback?**
A: Yes! This feedback is designed to support coaching conversations and can be discussed with instructors, coaches, or peers.

**Q: Will this appear on my transcript/record?**
A: Competency scores are tracked in your learner profile. Check with your institution about how simulation results factor into assessments.
